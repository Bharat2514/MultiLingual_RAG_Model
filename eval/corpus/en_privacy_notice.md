BHARAT TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED
CUSTOMER PRIVACY NOTICE

Version 2.0
Effective Date: 1 September 2024
Data Fiduciary: Bharat Technologies Private Limited

1. WHO WE ARE AND WHAT THIS NOTICE COVERS

Bharat Technologies Private Limited is the data fiduciary for the personal data described in this notice. This notice explains what personal data we collect from customers and prospective customers, why we process it, how long we keep it, and the rights available to you as a data principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

This notice covers our website, our customer portal and our mobile application. It does not cover the systems our enterprise customers operate themselves, where the customer is the data fiduciary and we act only as a data processor on their instructions.

2. THE PERSONAL DATA WE PROCESS

We process identity data, being your name, the name of your organisation and your designation. We process contact data, being your email address, telephone number and postal address. We process transaction data, being the products you have purchased, the invoices raised and the payments received. We process technical data, being the device type, the browser, the internet protocol address and the pages visited.

We do not knowingly collect the personal data of a child. Where we become aware that a user is under eighteen, the account is suspended and the data is deleted unless verifiable parental consent is obtained.

<!-- page -->

3. WHY WE PROCESS IT

We process identity, contact and transaction data to perform the contract with you, to raise invoices, to provide support and to meet our obligations under tax and company law. We process technical data to secure our services, to detect fraudulent access and to understand which parts of the product are used.

Where processing rests on your consent — marketing communication and non-essential analytics are the two cases — that consent is requested separately, is recorded with a timestamp, and may be withdrawn at any time without affecting the service you have paid for.

4. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Account data is retained for the life of the account and for three years after closure, so that a dispute raised within the limitation period can be answered. Invoices and supporting records are retained for eight years, which is the period prescribed by tax law. Support correspondence is retained for two years. Marketing consent records are retained for three years after the consent is withdrawn, because we must be able to prove that the withdrawal was honoured.

Technical logs of ordinary product usage are retained for one hundred and eighty days.

5. WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

We share personal data with payment processors, with cloud hosting providers, with our customer support platform and with our statutory auditors. Every such recipient is bound by a written contract that limits use to our instructions, requires equivalent security measures, and obliges notification of a breach within twenty four hours.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with advertising networks.

Where a recipient is located outside India, the transfer is made only to a country not restricted by the Central Government for this purpose, and the contractual protections above apply unchanged.

6. YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA PRINCIPAL

You have the right to obtain a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities undertaken. You have the right to correction of inaccurate data, to completion of incomplete data, and to updating of data that has changed. You have the right to erasure of data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. You have the right to nominate another individual to exercise these rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise a right, write to the Data Protection Officer at the address in section 8. We respond within thirty days. Where a request is manifestly unfounded or repetitive we may decline it, and we will give reasons in writing.

<!-- page -->

7. GRIEVANCES

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may raise a grievance with the Data Protection Officer, who will respond within thirty days of receiving it. If you remain unsatisfied after that, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

We maintain a register of grievances received and their outcomes, and the register is reviewed by the audit committee twice a year.

8. CONTACT

The Data Protection Officer may be contacted in writing at the registered office at Indore, or by electronic mail at the address published on the privacy page of our website. A request should state the right being exercised and enough information for us to identify the account; we may ask for further verification where an account cannot be identified, and we will not use that verification data for any other purpose.

9. SECURITY

We protect personal data with encryption in transit and at rest, with multi factor authentication on every administrative account, with access limited to staff who need it for their role, and with an annual independent security assessment. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of a personal data breach as required by law and without undue delay.

10. CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE

We may update this notice. Where a change materially affects how we process your personal data, we will give notice by electronic mail at least fifteen days before the change takes effect. The current version is always available on the privacy page of our website, together with the date it took effect.
