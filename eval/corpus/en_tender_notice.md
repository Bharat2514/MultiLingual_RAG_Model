INDORE MUNICIPAL CORPORATION
HEALTH AND SANITATION DEPARTMENT

NOTICE INVITING e-TENDER

NIT Number: IMC/HS/SWM/2024/31
Date of Publication: 5 August 2024
Mode: Online, through the state e-procurement portal

1. INVITATION

Online percentage rate tenders are invited from eligible contractors for door to door collection, transportation and secondary handling of municipal solid waste in Zone 14 and Zone 15 of the Indore Municipal Corporation area for a period of three years, extendable by two years on the same terms subject to satisfactory performance.

2. KEY DATES AND VALUES

The estimated contract value is forty two crore rupees for the three year period. The earnest money deposit is eighty four lakh rupees, being two percent of the estimated contract value. The tender document fee is fifteen thousand rupees and is non refundable.

The tender document may be downloaded from 5 August 2024. The last date for the submission of online bids is 2 September 2024 up to 17:30 hours. The technical bid will be opened on 4 September 2024 at 11:00 hours in the office of the Additional Commissioner. The financial bid of technically qualified bidders will be opened on a date notified separately, and bidders will be given at least three days notice.

Bids must remain valid for one hundred and twenty days from the date of opening of the technical bid.

<!-- page -->

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

3.1 Financial Capacity

The bidder must have an average annual turnover of not less than fourteen crore rupees in the last three audited financial years. The bidder must have a positive net worth in each of the last three financial years. A certificate from a practising chartered accountant, quoting the unique document identification number, must be enclosed.

3.2 Technical Capacity

The bidder must have successfully executed, in the last seven years, either one similar work of value not less than sixteen crore eighty lakh rupees, or two similar works each of value not less than ten crore fifty lakh rupees, or three similar works each of value not less than eight crore forty lakh rupees. Similar work means door to door solid waste collection in an urban local body serving a population of not less than five lakh.

The bidder must own or have committed access to a fleet of not fewer than sixty vehicles, of which at least forty must be less than five years old on the date of bid submission.

3.3 Disqualification

A bidder blacklisted by any urban local body or state government department on the date of bid submission is not eligible. A bidder who has abandoned a contract with the Corporation in the preceding five years is not eligible. Conditional bids are liable to be rejected without any reference to the bidder.

<!-- page -->

4. PERFORMANCE SECURITY AND PAYMENT

The successful bidder shall furnish a performance security equal to five percent of the accepted contract value within twenty one days of the issue of the letter of acceptance. The performance security shall be furnished in the form of a bank guarantee from a scheduled commercial bank, valid for the contract period plus a further six months.

Running account bills shall be submitted monthly. The Corporation shall release payment within thirty days of the certification of the bill by the Zonal Health Officer. Where payment is delayed beyond forty five days without a recorded reason, simple interest at the rate of eight percent per annum shall be payable to the contractor.

Two percent of each running account bill shall be retained as security deposit, and the accumulated retention shall be released twelve months after the completion of the contract, subject to a satisfactory performance certificate.

5. PENALTIES

A penalty of five thousand rupees per ward per day shall be levied where door to door collection is not carried out on any working day. A penalty of twenty five thousand rupees per instance shall be levied for the mixing of segregated waste during transportation. A penalty of one lakh rupees per instance shall be levied for the unauthorised disposal of waste outside the designated processing facility.

The cumulative penalty in any month shall not exceed ten percent of the monthly bill. Where the cumulative penalty exceeds ten percent of the monthly bill in three consecutive months, the Corporation may terminate the contract after issuing a notice of thirty days.

<!-- page -->

6. LABOUR AND SAFETY OBLIGATIONS

The contractor shall engage sanitation workers on its own rolls and shall pay wages not less than the minimum wages notified by the Labour Department of the state government. Wages shall be paid by bank transfer on or before the seventh day of the following month. The contractor shall enrol every worker under the Employees Provident Fund and the Employees State Insurance schemes and shall produce the monthly challans with each running account bill.

The contractor shall provide every sanitation worker with two sets of uniform, safety shoes, hand gloves, a mask and a reflective jacket every year. No worker shall be required to enter a sewer or a septic tank manually under any circumstance.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

A dispute arising out of the contract shall first be referred to the Commissioner of the Corporation, who shall give a decision within sixty days. Where the decision is not acceptable, the dispute shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed by the Principal Secretary, Urban Development and Housing Department. The seat of arbitration shall be Indore, and the proceedings shall be conducted in English or Hindi.

8. GENERAL

The Corporation reserves the right to accept or reject any or all bids without assigning any reason. Corrigenda, if any, shall be published only on the e-procurement portal, and bidders are advised to check the portal until the date of bid submission.

Additional Commissioner
Indore Municipal Corporation
