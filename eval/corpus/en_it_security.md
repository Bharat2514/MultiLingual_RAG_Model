BHARAT TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED
INFORMATION SECURITY AND ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

Document Reference: IT/SEC/2024/02
Version: 4.1
Effective Date: 1 July 2024
Owner: Chief Information Security Officer

1. SCOPE

This policy governs the use of all information systems owned, leased or operated by the company, including laptops, mobile devices, cloud services, source code repositories and customer data. It applies to employees, interns, contractors and third party vendors who are granted access to company systems.

Acceptance of this policy is recorded during onboarding, and re-acknowledgement is required annually. Access is provisioned only after acknowledgement is recorded.

2. DATA CLASSIFICATION

Information is classified into four levels. Public information may be shared without restriction. Internal information may be shared within the company but not outside it. Confidential information may be shared only with named individuals who have a business need to know. Restricted information, which includes customer personal data, payment credentials and unreleased financial results, requires the written approval of the data owner before any sharing.

Every document, repository and dataset must carry its classification label. An unlabelled artefact is treated as Confidential by default.

3. ACCESS CONTROL

3.1 Authentication

Multi factor authentication is mandatory for every system that holds Confidential or Restricted information, and for all administrative accounts without exception. Passwords must be at least fourteen characters long and are checked against a list of known breached credentials at the time they are set.

Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule. Rotation is required only where there is evidence or reasonable suspicion of compromise, which reflects current guidance from the National Institute of Standards and Technology.

3.2 Privileged Access

Administrative access is granted for a maximum of eight hours at a time through the just in time access system, and every elevation is logged with the business justification. Standing administrative access is permitted only for two designated break glass accounts, whose credentials are held in a sealed envelope in the company safe and whose use triggers an immediate alert to the Chief Information Security Officer.

3.3 Access Review

The system owner reviews the access list of every Confidential and Restricted system once each quarter. Access that has not been used in ninety days is revoked automatically. On separation, all access is revoked within four hours of the last working hour, and on involuntary separation access is revoked before the employee is informed.

4. DEVICE SECURITY

Full disk encryption is mandatory on every laptop and is verified by the endpoint management agent. A device that has not checked in to the management console for thirty days is blocked from the corporate network until it is re-enrolled.

Personal devices may be used to access company email and chat only through the managed application container. Source code must never be cloned onto a personal device. The screen lock timeout must not exceed five minutes.

The loss or theft of any device holding company information must be reported to the security operations centre within two hours of discovery, at any hour of the day.

5. ACCEPTABLE USE

Company systems may be used for incidental personal purposes, provided such use does not interfere with work, does not incur cost to the company and does not involve prohibited content. The following are prohibited without exception: attempting to bypass a security control, sharing an individual account credential with another person, installing unlicensed software, and uploading company source code or customer data to a public artificial intelligence service or any personal cloud storage account.

The use of approved artificial intelligence coding assistants is permitted on repositories classified as Internal or below, and is prohibited on repositories classified as Confidential or Restricted.

6. INCIDENT RESPONSE

A suspected security incident must be reported to the security operations centre immediately, and in no case later than one hour after discovery. Reporting is required even where the reporter believes the incident may be a false alarm; the company operates a no blame reporting culture and no disciplinary action follows from a good faith report.

Incidents are triaged into four severities. A severity one incident, which is a confirmed breach of Restricted data or a loss of production availability affecting all customers, requires the incident commander to be appointed within fifteen minutes and the executive team to be notified within thirty minutes. A severity two incident requires an incident commander within one hour. Severity three and four incidents are handled within the normal working day.

Where personal data of a data principal is breached, the Data Protection Officer notifies the Data Protection Board and the affected data principals as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and in any event without undue delay.

7. VENDOR MANAGEMENT

A vendor that will process company Confidential or Restricted information must complete a security assessment before contract signature and must be re-assessed every two years. The contract must include a right to audit, a breach notification obligation of twenty four hours, and a commitment to delete company data within thirty days of contract termination.

8. TRAINING AND ENFORCEMENT

Every employee completes security awareness training within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter. Simulated phishing exercises are run at least once each quarter. An employee who fails three consecutive simulated phishing exercises is enrolled in mandatory additional training.

A wilful violation of this policy is treated as misconduct and is dealt with under the disciplinary procedure in the employee handbook. A violation that causes a breach of customer data may result in termination.
