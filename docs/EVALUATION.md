# Evaluation

What has been measured, how, and what the numbers do and do not show. The
harness, gold set and corpus are described in [`eval/README.md`](../eval/README.md);
every number below comes from [`eval/results/tables.md`](../eval/results/tables.md),
generated by `python eval/run_eval.py` on 2026-09-02.

## Setup

- **Corpus:** 16 hand-written documents (8 English, 8 Hindi), 41 chunks. Six carry
  gold labels; ten are *topically adjacent* distractors — a procurement manual next
  to the tender notice, an RTI circular next to the pension circular.
- **Gold set:** 182 questions. 150 are scored for retrieval; `summary` (8) and
  `adversarial` (24) questions are excluded from retrieval metrics because every
  chunk is relevant to a summary and none to an out-of-scope question.
- **Relevance:** anchor substrings, not chunk ids, so labels survive chunk-size sweeps.
- **Models:** `bge-m3` (dense + learned sparse), `bge-reranker-v2-m3`, `top_k = 20`.

## Retrieval ablation (n = 150)

| arm | Hit@1 | Hit@5 | Δ Hit@5 vs dense | 95% CI | MRR | nDCG@5 | P95 ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| dense only | 0.667 | 0.873 | ±0.0 | 0.81–0.92 | 0.759 | 0.778 | 61 |
| sparse only | 0.567 | 0.653 | −22.0 | 0.57–0.72 | 0.607 | 0.613 | 49 |
| dense + sparse (weighted RRF) | 0.513 | 0.720 | −15.3 | 0.64–0.79 | 0.606 | 0.626 | 56 |
| dense + rerank | **0.893** | **0.973** | +10.0 | 0.93–0.99 | **0.929** | **0.939** | 8 802 |
| hybrid + rerank (shipped) | 0.887 | 0.960 | +8.7 | 0.92–0.98 | 0.918 | 0.928 | 12 837 |
| shipped − Hinglish normalisation | 0.887 | 0.960 | +8.7 | 0.92–0.98 | 0.920 | 0.930 | 11 479 |

**What this shows**

1. **The reranker is the whole game.** It lifts Hit@1 from 0.67 to 0.89. Retrieval
   optimises recall; the cross-encoder turns that recall into precision.
2. **The sparse arm hurts on this corpus.** Lexical overlap between a Hindi question
   and an English passage is near zero, so sparse adds noise on exactly the
   cross-lingual queries this product exists for. After reranking the difference
   between dense+rerank and hybrid+rerank (0.973 vs 0.960 Hit@5) is two questions,
   inside the confidence interval — not enough to call, but it does not favour hybrid.
3. **Rerank latency is the cost.** P95 of 9–13 s here is CPU fp32 in the eval run;
   on Apple-silicon MPS the warm rerank is ~0.3 s (see the README's latency table).

## Hinglish normalisation

| configuration | n | Hit@1 | Hit@5 | MRR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| with normalisation | 32 | 0.781 | 1.000 | 0.867 |
| without (romanized only) | 32 | 0.781 | 1.000 | 0.875 |

**No measurable benefit on this set.** bge-m3 already embeds romanized Hindi well
enough that the Devanagari variant adds nothing for these 32 questions. That is a
real finding, not a failure to report: the transliteration path costs an extra
embedding and search per query. Before keeping or removing it, the Hinglish slice
needs harder cases — heavier code-mixing, SMS spellings ("kitna" / "ktna"),
misspellings — where a learned transliterator (IndicXlit) could be compared with
the current rule-based one.

## Not yet measured

- **End-to-end generation** — RAGAS faithfulness and answer relevancy, language
  fidelity, anti-hallucination correctness on the 24 adversarial questions.
  `make eval-full` runs these, but needs a billed LLM key: it issues thousands of
  calls, and the free Gemini tier throttles it to days.
- **Claim-check calibration** — how often the per-sentence verifier
  (`app/intelligence/claims.py`) agrees with a human on supported vs unsupported,
  with and without the cross-encoder.
- **The CI baseline** — `eval/results/baseline.json`, which the `eval-gate`
  workflow compares against, has to be produced by that workflow on CI hardware
  (CPU fp32), not locally; see the comment at the top of `.github/workflows/eval-gate.yml`.

## Reproducing

```bash
make infra && make install-ml && make download-models
make eval-validate   # gold labels resolve (seconds, no models)
make eval            # retrieval ablation grid → eval/results/
make eval-full       # + generation and the LLM judge (billed key)
```
